You're not alone.

If you're not feeling safe right now, please consider taking one of these steps:

  • Reach out to a trusted person - a parent, friend, teacher, or someone you trust.
  • Contact your local emergency services or a crisis helpline if you are in immediate danger.
  • Try to move to a place where you feel safe.

MindMate is not able to provide emergency support directly, but you deserve real help - please reach out to someone right now.
