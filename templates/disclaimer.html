About MindMate

MindMate provides general wellness and productivity support. It is not a medical or mental-health diagnostic service, and it does not diagnose depression, anxiety, or any other condition.

The suggestions provided are general in nature and are not a substitute for professional medical or psychological advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to a trusted person or a qualified professional.
